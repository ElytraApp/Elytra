### Improvements

• The sidebar feels more alive. Unread counts fade in and out, favicons appear as they load, and the sync bar slides smoothly into place.

### Fixes

• The Solo and Folders widgets can pick a feed or a folder again. The picker lists every feed and folder, and you can search it.
• "Show Only Unread" in the Folders widget now filters the list as intended.
• Widgets that are already on your desktop keep their place, but they reset to their default options. Open the widget settings to pick your feed or folder again.
